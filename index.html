Participant Information

Please read this sheet carefully before deciding whether to take part.

Thank you for your interest in this study.

What is this study about?

We are interested in studying consumer behaviour in young adults.

Who is running it?

This study is being done by the students of Master of Arts (Psychology), University of Delhi, as part of the Human Cognition practical (Practical Group 1, Subgroup 1), under the supervision of Dr. Dinesh Chhabra.

What will you be asked to do?

  1. Understanding the Task. You will see four decks of cards labelled A, B, C, and D on the computer screen. Your task is to select cards from these decks to earn as much virtual money as possible.
  2. Selecting a Card. You may begin by selecting a card from any one of the four decks. You are free to choose whichever deck you prefer. To draw a card from that deck, you will tap on Play. To select a different deck you will tap on Pass.
  3. Understanding Gains and Losses. Each time you select a card, you will receive a monetary gain. However, some selections may also result in a monetary loss. The amounts gained or lost may vary between selections.
  4. Observing the Feedback. After each selection, the computer will display the outcome of your choice and your updated virtual monetary balance. Pay attention to this information while making subsequent decisions.
  5. Viewing the Pictures. Different pictures will appear on the card decks throughout the task. These pictures are part of the activity. You do not need to evaluate the pictures or respond to them separately.
  6. Continuing Your Selections. You may continue selecting cards from the same deck or switch to another deck at any point. There is no fixed order in which the decks must be selected.
  7. Maximizing Your Earnings. Your objective is to earn as much virtual money as possible by the end of the task. Make your decisions independently, using the feedback from previous selections as you consider appropriate.
  8. Completing the Task. Continue selecting cards until the computer indicates that the task is complete. The money used in this activity is hypothetical; you will not gain or lose any actual money.

How long will it take?

The session will take approximately 25 minutes.

Do you have to take part?

No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any penalty, simply by telling the researcher or closing the window.

Are there any risks?

There are no known risks beyond the mild eye strain that can come from any screen-based task.

What happens to your data?

Your responses are recorded anonymously. You will be asked to write a participant ID, wherein you can write your pseudo (or false) name or can leave the column blank for the system to generate a Participant ID for you. Data, so collected, will be stored securely in a password-protected online database, accessible only to the research team, and used only for academic purposes, including class reports and possible publication, in a form that cannot identify you. Post the completion of the study, the data will be permanently deleted from our systems.

Will you be told the full aim of the study?

Yes. At the end of the session we will explain the study in full and answer any questions. Some details are kept general until then so that they do not influence how you perform the task.
