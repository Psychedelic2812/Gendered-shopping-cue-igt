Modified Iowa Gambling Task

Trial -
Balance ₹1,90,000

Click Start to begin.

You start with ₹1,90,000. On each turn an arrow will point to one deck. Press PLAY (A key) to take a card from that deck, which may win or lose money, or PASS (L key) to skip it. You have 4 seconds to decide. Try to finish with as much money as possible. Click Start when you are ready.
